TASK B — HOW PEOPLE WORK WITH THE SYSTEM

A provisional Task A record is given after the paper. Check everything against the paper itself.

First write Workflow_Summary: two or three plain sentences describing who does what with the focal system, in order.

Then answer the questions. For EACH question fill three fields, IN THIS ORDER:
_quote: copy a SHORT passage (at most 20 words) WORD FOR WORD from the paper that supports your answer. Do not paraphrase, shorten with "...", or join separate sentences. If no passage supports a YES, write NONE.
_page: the number of the PAGE marker where the quote appears, or NONE.
_answer: as listed for that question.
Answer YES only if your quote clearly states it. Answer NO if the paper shows it is not the case, including when the reported workflow is fully automatic. Answer NOT_REPORTED if the paper does not say.

B01_Person_Uses_System (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The paper describes a person directly using the focal system: drawing into it and receiving its results. People who only drew the training or test data, or who only rated outputs afterwards, do NOT count.
B02_Several_People_Same_Workflow (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): Two or more people take part in the SAME sketching workflow, for example drawing together, sharing sketches, or reviewing each other's sketches. Several study participants working separately do NOT count.
B03_Responds_While_Drawing (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The system acts on the drawing while the person is still drawing, updating its response as strokes are added.
B04_Designed_For_Incomplete_Sketch (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The system is explicitly designed to take an unfinished or partial sketch as input.
B05_Person_Acts_On_Result (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): After the system shows a result, the person responds to that result (edits, redraws, selects, corrects or refines) and the system responds again. Loops inside an algorithm, training iterations, or running a model on many test sketches do NOT count.
B06_Loop_Purpose (CORRECT_OR_CONFIRM / REFINE_TOWARDS_TARGET / EXPLORE_ALTERNATIVES / NOT_APPLICABLE / UNCLEAR): Only if B05 is YES, otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE.
  CORRECT_OR_CONFIRM: the system interprets or checks the drawing and the person fixes or confirms it.
  REFINE_TOWARDS_TARGET: the person adjusts the sketch or result step by step towards a result they already intend.
  EXPLORE_ALTERNATIVES: the system offers new, different or divergent material that the person can take up, compare or recombine.
B07_Undo_History_Versions (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The person can undo, view a history, or return to earlier versions of the SAME piece of work.
B08_Own_Earlier_Sketches (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The person can save, load, browse or reuse their OWN earlier separate sketches. A search database of other people's sketches does NOT count.
B09_Kept_Beyond_Session (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): Sketch material is stored so that it can be used in a later session, not only during the current one.
B10_Several_Results_Shown (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): For one input, the person is shown several alternative results, candidates or interpretations, including a ranked list.
B11_Separate_Branches_Kept (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): Two or more separate development paths from a common earlier state are kept, each available for further work.
B12_Evaluation_With_People (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The paper reports a study in which people used or judged the system: user study, participants, interviews, questionnaires, workshops, or expert review.
B13_Evaluation_Focus (USABILITY_EXPERIENCE / OUTPUT_QUALITY / TASK_PERFORMANCE / PROCESS_OF_IDEAS_OR_DRAWING / PRACTICE_DEPLOYMENT / NOT_APPLICABLE): Only if B12 is YES, otherwise NOT_APPLICABLE. Choose the MAIN thing the study with people examines.
  USABILITY_EXPERIENCE: ease of use, satisfaction, workload, preference for the interface.
  OUTPUT_QUALITY: how good, realistic or faithful the results look.
  TASK_PERFORMANCE: speed or accuracy in completing a task.
  PROCESS_OF_IDEAS_OR_DRAWING: how people's ideas or drawings developed during the activity.
  PRACTICE_DEPLOYMENT: use in real professional or everyday practice over time.
B14_Claims_Creative_Support (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): The authors explicitly claim the system supports creativity, ideation, exploration, design thinking, or artistic practice.
B15_Artists_Or_Designers (YES/NO/NOT_REPORTED): Artists, illustrators, animators, designers or architects are the intended users or the study participants.

COMMON TRAPS
- An automatic classifier evaluated on a dataset: B01 is NO, and B05 is NO.
- A retrieval system showing top results: B10 is YES.
- Redrawing to make a new query without the system building on the previous result: B05 is usually NO.
- Several participants each using the system alone: B02 is NO.
- A model that stores features or embeddings: B07, B08 and B09 are NO unless the person can get their sketches back.
